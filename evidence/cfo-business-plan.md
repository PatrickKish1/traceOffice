# TraceOffice business plan

This is the plan to enter in cfo.ai. It is not a live export. cfo.ai still needs a signed-in workspace before these rows exist there. The MCP server is configured at `https://api.cfo.ai/mcp` in `.cursor/mcp.json`.

TraceOffice is free while it runs on the developer's machine. The paid offer is a hosted control room: one office for a team's coding agents, with Neatlogs as the trace store.

Illustrative monthly model. These figures are planning assumptions, not measured revenue.

| | Base | Growth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | 40 teams | 120 teams |
| Price | $49 / team / month | $49 / team / month |
| Revenue | $1,960 | $5,880 |
| Neatlogs + hosting | $350 | $900 |
| Support | $400 | $800 |
| Gross profit | $1,210 | $4,180 |
| Gross margin | 62% | 71% |

Runway assumption: one founder, $4,000 monthly operating cost before revenue. At the base case, gross profit does not cover that cost. At the growth case, gross profit covers it with about $180 left. The plan is not to hire until the growth case is real.

Pricing stays at one seat price so the office is the product and Neatlogs remains the usage-based observability bill underneath it.
